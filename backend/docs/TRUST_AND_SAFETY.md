# Trust and safety: the rules of the platform

Goal (owner, Oct 2026): a safe, transparent service where nobody can steal, cheat or lie without it being seen, with strict
rules that apply to everyone, automatic temporary blocks, and a fair path to staff. Sellers, buyers and drivers each see
exactly what they need, nothing more.

Honest limit: no system makes fraud impossible. This one makes every important step **proved by two people** (a code or a
QR scan) and **recorded forever** (history that nobody can edit), so that a dispute can be decided from evidence, and
repeat offenders are stopped.

## What we learned from others (research, Oct 2026)
| Problem seen elsewhere | Who solved it, how | Our rule |
|---|---|---|
| Courier marks "delivered" without delivering, or keeps the cash | Uber, GoSend: delivery PIN given by the receiver | Delivery only with the receiver's 4-digit code or QR scan; cash checked before "delivered" |
| Receiver cannot follow the parcel without the app | GrabExpress, GoSend, Uber: tracking link opened in a browser, shared by the sender (WhatsApp) | Secret tracking link per order: live map, time left, amount to pay, the code/QR; ends 24 h after the end |
| "The parcel was already damaged / it was swapped" | Lalamove: photo proof required, clear rules for valid photos | Driver must photograph the parcel at pickup (shown to sender and receiver); handover from the sender confirmed with a pickup code |
| Fake "attempts", far-away confirmations | Proof-of-delivery industry: GPS check 50-200 m | Driver's position is recorded at pickup and delivery; more than 300 m away = flagged for staff |
| Buyers refuse cash-on-delivery parcels (Algeria: 20-40 % returns) | Algerian sellers: confirm the buyer before sending | Receiver can press "I am expecting this parcel" on the tracking page; senders see a receiver number's past deliveries/refusals (counts only) |
| Receiver says "wrong item / damaged" after accepting | Flipkart Open-Box: inspect BEFORE giving the code; giving the code = accepting | Seller chooses "may open before paying" or "outside check only"; the page says clearly: give the code only when satisfied |
| Banned people come back with a new account | inDrive: manual + automatic re-registration checks; device fingerprints | Banned Google account, phone device, driver ID-card number are blocked for new accounts |
| Driver lends his account to someone else | inDrive: periodic photo checks | a selfie before going online, once a week, kept next to the ID photo for staff to compare (no automatic face matching: that needs a paid service); vehicle photo at sign-up |
| Unfair automatic bans with no explanation | Gig workers' main complaint (Uber/DoorDash); California law: written reason + human appeal | Automatic blocks are TEMPORARY only; every penalty shows its reason and evidence; one appeal per penalty; permanent bans only by a person |
| Dangerous or illegal goods | Uber: prohibited-items list, driver may refuse | Prohibited list accepted at sign-up; driver may refuse at pickup without penalty (reason + photo) |
| Emergencies on the road | inDrive: SOS button | "Emergency" button on the driver's job screen: alerts staff at once with the position, and calls 17 (police), 14 (civil protection), 1055 (gendarmerie); numbers to be confirmed locally |

## The life of an order (who proves what)
1. **Sender creates the order**: pickup, drop-off, receiver name and phone, size, cash to collect, inspection choice, price.
   The sender gets a **tracking link** to send to the receiver (one tap opens WhatsApp with the message ready; free for us).
2. **No waiting for the receiver** (owner's decision, Oct 2026): couriers are searched as soon as the order exists. On the
   link the receiver may say "I am expecting it" (optional; the sender and driver see it) or report a problem.
3. **Couriers answer, the customer chooses**: nearby couriers see the price, accept it or offer more (never above the maximum);
   the customer picks one (price, time to arrive, rating, deliveries) and the price locks. The sender sees the courier's name,
   vehicle, rating; the receiver's page too.
4. **Pickup**: the sender shows a **pickup code / QR**; the driver scans or types it, takes a **photo of the parcel**,
   and the phone's position is recorded. Without the code and photo the parcel is not "picked up".
   The driver may refuse a parcel (prohibited, too big, badly packed) with a reason and photo: no penalty.
5. **On the way**: sender and receiver see the driver on the map with the time left. If the driver's phone goes silent for
   20 minutes with a parcel, staff are alerted; after 3 hours a "parcel overdue" case opens.
6. **At the door**: the receiver checks the parcel (opens it if the seller allowed it), pays the cash shown on his page,
   and only then gives the **delivery code or shows the QR**. Giving the code means "I received it and paid".
   The driver confirms the cash amount, scans/types the code; position recorded.
7. **Refused at the door** (damaged, wrong item, not ordered, changed mind, cannot pay, nobody there, wrong address):
   the driver chooses the reason (+ photo for damaged/wrong item); the parcel automatically goes back to the sender
   (return leg) and staff get a case. The sender confirms getting it back with the return code.
8. **After**: both sides rate each other. Sender or receiver can report a problem within 24 hours (with photos).
   Every step above is in the order's history, visible to the sender (and the parts that concern them to driver and receiver).

## Cancelling
- Before a driver accepts: the sender cancels freely.
- After a driver accepts, before pickup: the sender may cancel (reason asked); a cancel more than 3 minutes after
  acceptance counts as a **late cancel**. The driver may give the job back (reason asked): it returns to searching for
  another driver at the same price, and counts as a **driver cancel**.
- After pickup: nobody can cancel. The sender may **call the parcel back** (it returns to him), the receiver may refuse it.

## The rules ("laws") and their points
Points count for 90 days, per profile (a sender profile and a driver profile are separate).
| Rule broken | Points | Who decides |
|---|---|---|
| Driver gives back a job after accepting | 1 | automatic |
| Sender cancels late | 1 | automatic |
| Driver's confirmation far from the place, confirmed false by staff | 3 | staff |
| Receiver refused: "I never ordered this" (fake order) | 2 | automatic (sender) |
| Wrong or changed item, confirmed by staff | 4 | staff (sender or driver) |
| Parcel damaged by the driver, confirmed by staff | 4 | staff |
| Rude, threatening or unsafe behaviour, confirmed by staff | 3-6 | staff |
| Prohibited goods sent | 6 | staff |
| Cash kept / parcel stolen, confirmed by staff | ban | staff (person) |

What points do (automatically):
| Points in 90 days | Result |
|---|---|
| 3 | written warning (notification + shown in the app) |
| 5 | blocked 24 hours |
| 8 | blocked 7 days, staff review opened |
| 12 | blocked until staff decide |
Plus: staff may block for a set time or **ban permanently** (always with a written reason). A ban closes every profile of
the account and blocks its Google account, its phone devices and, for drivers, the ID-card number from making a new account.

Fairness: every penalty shows the person the rule, the order and the evidence. Each penalty can be appealed once (text);
a staff member decides and the answer is shown. Cancelled penalties remove their points and their block.

## What each person sees
- **Sender (seller)**: everything about his order, the driver's name/vehicle/rating/position, the parcel photo, the full history, the receiver's page link, the receiver number's past deliveries and refusals (counts only).
- **Receiver (buyer)**, through the link, no account needed: status, map with the driver and time left, driver's first name/vehicle/rating, the parcel photo, the amount to pay, the inspection rule, the code and QR, "I am expecting it", "report a problem". The link ends 24 hours after the order ends.
- **Driver**: before accepting: price, distances, cash to collect, sender rating. After accepting: addresses, names, phones; never the delivery code.
- **Staff**: everything, including comments and evidence, never the delivery code; every staff action is recorded.

## Privacy and limits
- The driver's position is shown only during an active job, and only to that job's sender and receiver.
- Receiver-number statistics show counts only, no names or addresses. (Legal review needed before launch: data protection is parked.)
- Device and ID-card numbers are stored only as fingerprints (one-way codes), never as the numbers themselves, except the last 3 ID digits for staff.
- Phone numbers typed by Google accounts are not verified: they never decide anything on their own.

## Built (Oct 2026)
1. Proof chain: tracking link + receiver page, pickup code + parcel photo, delivery QR, positions recorded, refusal reasons, driver gives job back, sender calls parcel back, inspection choice, "I am expecting it", receiver-number history.
2. Rules engine: points, automatic blocks, warnings, appeals, bans (account, Google, device, ID-card number), rules accepted at sign-up, staff console page.
3. Safety nets: silent-driver, overdue and late-pickup cases; problem reports with up to 3 photos within 24 h; emergency button; weekly selfie before going online.

## Not built (needs money or a decision)
- Automatic face matching of selfies (paid services), Google Play Integrity checks (needs the Google Play account, $25), insurance or compensation for lost goods (finance and law are parked).
